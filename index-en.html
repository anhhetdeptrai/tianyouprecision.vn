ABOUT US

Tian-You Precision (Vietnam) Co., Ltd.

Tian You Precision Co., Ltd. originates from its parent company, founded in Taiwan in 1972. Building on more than 50 years of manufacturing experience, we established Tian You Vietnam in 2006 with registered capital of USD 2 million and a team of over 170 people, specializing in sheet metal fabrication, tube laser cutting, metal stamping, robotic welding, CNC tube bending and automated powder coating.

Our Vietnam factory exports about 90% of its output — mainly to the United States and Japan — serving customers across many industries, including retail, home appliances and automotive.

✔ Household, storage & display — metal furniture, storage solutions and display racks for retail and household markets
✔ Industrial — components and assemblies for retail shelving, home appliances and automotive applications
