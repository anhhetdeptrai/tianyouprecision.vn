History & Mission

From Taiwan in 1972 to Vietnam today, we are committed to raising the standard of metal manufacturing worldwide.

Our Story

With over 50 years of experience, Tian You is a leading OEM/ODM manufacturing partner in professional metal fabrication

1972

🇹🇼 Founded in Taiwan

Tian You Precision Co., Ltd. originates from its parent company, founded in Taiwan in 1972 and specializing in metal stamping and plastic injection.

A journey of over 50 years: Building on long manufacturing experience to grow into a professional OEM manufacturer.

2006

🇻🇳 Tian You Vietnam Established

We established Tian You Vietnam, with registered capital of USD 2 million and a team of over 170 people, to expand production capacity and better serve a growing base of international customers.

The factory is equipped with advanced capabilities including tube laser cutting, CNC tube bending, robotic welding and automated powder coating.

Today

🌍 A Trusted OEM Partner

Tian You Vietnam exports about 90% of its output — mainly to the United States and Japan — with 10% serving the domestic market.

Grounded in the core values of customer focus, employee care and continuous improvement, Tian You has become a trusted OEM partner for customers in retail, home appliances and automotive.

Production Process

01

📋 Inquiry & Consultation

Customers send technical drawings or product descriptions. Our engineers analyze the requirements, propose the best solution and agree on production terms.

✓ DFM (Design for Manufacturability) analysis
✓ Detailed quotation
✓ Production planning
02

🛠️ Tooling Preparation (if required)

We have the equipment and people to supervise and verify that every die is suitable and meets quality requirements for the product.

✓ Accurate die suitability check
✓ Process optimization
✓ Feasibility check
03

🔧 Machine & Equipment Setup

We set up the right machines — punch presses, 3D CNC laser cutters, hydraulic benders or welding robots — depending on the product.

✓ Machine condition check
✓ Precision calibration
✓ CNC programming as needed
04

📦 Incoming Material Inspection

All incoming materials (steel, aluminum, copper, plastics) are quality-inspected to international standards before use.

✓ Chemical composition check
✓ Hardness & ductility testing
✓ Appropriate material selection
05

⚙️ Sample Production

Before mass production, we make samples so customers can check quality, dimensions and accuracy.

✓ Small-batch samples
✓ Full quality inspection
✓ Adjustments if needed
06

🏭 Mass Production

Once the customer approves the sample, we start high-speed mass production under strict quality control.

✓ Stamping / laser cutting / bending to spec
✓ In-process inspection at every step
✓ Daily progress reports
07

🔄 Surface Treatment & Finishing

Depending on requirements, we carry out welding, bending, powder coating, plating or other protective finishes.

✓ Professional TIG/MIG welding
✓ Automated powder coating
✓ Component assembly
08

✅ Final Quality Inspection

Every finished product goes through final quality inspection to ensure it meets international standards.

✓ Dimensional & accuracy check (CMM)
✓ Visual inspection
✓ Durability & performance testing
09

📦 Packing & Shipping

Products are safely packed to customer requirements (foam, carton, pallet) and ready for export.

✓ Protective packaging
✓ Labeling & lot traceability
✓ Scheduled shipping
