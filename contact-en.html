Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Does Tian-You Precision offer product design support?

Yes. Our professional engineers (CAD, SolidWorks, etc.) are ready to advise on and design products to customer requirements.

❓ What is the lead time?

It depends on volume and product complexity. Typically 30-45 days from order receipt.

❓ Do you support international export?

Yes. We have experience exporting to Taiwan, Japan, the US, the EU and other markets.

❓ Do you hold international quality certifications?

Yes. We hold ISO 9001:2015, SMETA 4P, CTPAT and other industry certifications.

❓ Can I see samples before mass production?

Yes. We always provide samples for customer approval before large-volume production.

❓ What payment terms do you accept?

We accept several payment methods: T/T (bank transfer), L/C, D/A and D/P, depending on customer requirements.
